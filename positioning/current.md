## Hero angle

**"Organised freelancers earn more."**

Why: vague, non-specific claims are lower Meta-compliance risk than specific ones, and the angle ties the whole product (organisation) to the outcome freelancers care about (earning) without fabricating a number. Note: this is a plausibility claim, not a measured stat. We have no conversion data yet confirming it outperforms alternatives — that's what the ad test (Arm A vs Arm B) is for.

## Primary differentiator

**File approvals via shareable link, no client login required.** Clients review and comment without creating an account.

Why: last research (July 2026) found the common real alternative is a DIY stack (Notion + Trello/Asana + FreshBooks/Wave + spreadsheets), and client-facing review/approval is the piece a DIY stack handles worst (email chains, version confusion). This should be foregrounded in copy, not buried in a feature grid.

## Pillars

1. **One place instead of four tools** — CRM with Kanban pipeline, projects, time tracking, invoicing, notes-to-tasks. Positioned against the DIY stack, not against named paid competitors.
2. **Client approvals without friction** — the differentiator. No-login review link.
3. **Built for one person** — single-seat product, $29/mo, 15-day free trial. Sits inside the $20–66/mo band for paid all-in-ones.
