Getting Started
Sign Up & Create Your Account

Onboarding (4-Step Process)
After confirming your email, you'll complete onboarding:
Step 1: What do you do? Choose your role:
Freelancer, Consultant, Designer, Developer/Engineer, Agency, Coach/Trainer, Photographer/Videographer, or Other
Step 2: What do you want to use first? Select your primary need:
Dashboard, Clients, Projects, Time Tracking, Notes, Invoices, Client Approvals, or Notifications
Step 3: Your Business (Optional)
Business Name (required to continue)
Currency (searchable dropdown with ~80 currencies)
Step 4: Choose Your Plan
15-day free trial (no charge today)
Monthly: $29/month
Annual: $290/year (saves 2 months vs monthly)
Click Continue to Payment → Stripe checkout
After payment, automatically redirected to Dashboard
What You Get:
Unlimited clients & projects
Time tracking
Invoicing
Client approvals
Notes & notifications
Dashboard analytics
Subscription & Trial
Trial Access:
15 days from checkout

Best Practices
Based on the UI design:
✅ Complete your business profile - Invoice warnings appear if missing business name, email, address, or bank details
✅ Use billable vs non-billable time - Dashboard tracks "unbilled hours" for accurate client billing
✅ Follow invoice lifecycle - Draft → Sent → Paid (editing becomes restricted after sending)
✅ Set follow-up actions on clients - Surfaces on Dashboard to keep relationships active
✅ Organize with folders - Available for notes and reviews to keep content organized
✅ Use tags - On clients and notes for flexible filtering
✅ Monitor storage - Check /settings/storage regularly; delete old review files when done
✅ Set notification preferences - Control what emails you receive in /settings/notifications
✅ Leverage search - Quick access to any client, project, invoice, task, or note from Dashboard
Newly Added Workflows: Services + Proposals
12. Services (/services)
Purpose: Build a reusable catalog of service offerings (one-time or recurring).
Create Service: Name, description, default price, currency, recurring toggle (monthly/annually), and default task list.
Edit/Delete: Manage catalog entries from the Services table.
Usage: Services can be added to proposals as snapshot line items.
13. Proposals (/proposals, /proposals/:id, /proposal/:token)
Proposals list: Search by identifier/client/project, status filters, create/duplicate/delete actions.
Create Proposal modal: Select client, optional project, helper text for identifier, quick actions to create new client/project.
Proposal editor tabs:
Details: Client/project selection, identifier, cover image, About, Objective, validity preview.
Services: Add from catalog or manually, quantity/price, subtotal/discount/total calculations.
Conditions: Availability, project duration, payment structure/methods, optional notes, installment description when applicable.
Preview: Live client-facing render, copy link, open client view, save-and-send.
Public client view: Read-only proposal presentation with accept action and CTA contact options.
Status lifecycle: Draft -> Sent -> Read -> Accepted.

Purpose: Configure default values automatically applied when creating a new proposal.
