## What this says overall

The loudest pains split into two clusters: **feedback/approval chaos** (1, 2, 3, 7 — the approvals feature's home turf) and **money admin** (4, 10, 11). Lance's differentiator sits squarely on the loudest cluster, and uniquely connects the two (the person chasing feedback is the same person chasing the invoice). Pain 9 (contracts) is the clearest gap vs. paid competitors.
